Frequently Asked Questions About Other Home Appliances

Which water purifier is best for Kathmandu's tap water?
For Kathmandu's water quality, we recommend RO+UV purifiers like Kent Grand Plus or CG RO Pure. These remove dissolved impurities, heavy metals, and microorganisms effectively. Our team offers free water testing to help you choose the right model.
What capacity geyser should I buy for a family of 4?
A 15-liter geyser is sufficient for a family of 4, providing hot water for bathing and washing. For larger families or high usage, consider 25-30 liter models. We stock both sizes at competitive prices.
Are solar water heaters effective during winter in Nepal?
Yes, modern solar water heaters with vacuum tube technology work efficiently even on cloudy winter days. They can heat water up to 60-80 degrees Celsius. However, for consistent performance, consider a hybrid model with electric backup for prolonged cloudy periods.
