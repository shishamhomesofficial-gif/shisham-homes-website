Vacuum Cleaner Guide: Choose the Right Model for Your Home

From daily dust to deep weekend cleaning, a good vacuum cleaner saves time and keeps your indoor environment healthier. In Kathmandu homes, where fine dust can settle quickly, suction quality and maintenance convenience make a big difference.

Shisham Homes offers reliable vacuum cleaner options designed for living rooms, bedrooms, curtains, and corner cleaning. We focus on models that provide dependable motor performance and practical accessories for common household needs.

How to pick the right vacuum cleaner

  • Motor performance: Higher wattage generally supports stronger suction for stubborn dust and debris.
  • Bag or bagless design: Bag-type units can be hygienic for disposal; compare according to your routine.
  • Attachments: Crevice and brush tools help clean sofas, window tracks, and difficult corners.
  • Noise and handling: Lightweight bodies and smooth wheels are easier for regular home use.

Cleaning routine tips

  • Vacuum high-use areas 2-3 times weekly to control dust buildup.
  • Clean or replace filters based on usage so airflow stays strong.
  • Use the right attachment for fabric surfaces to avoid wear.
  • Store the machine in a dry area and keep cables untangled after use.
