Types of Water Purifiers Available

  • RO (Reverse Osmosis) Purifiers – Best for removing dissolved salts, heavy metals, and chemicals. Ideal for areas with high TDS (total dissolved solids) water. Most Kent and CG models use RO technology.
  • UV (Ultraviolet) Purifiers – Kills bacteria and viruses without removing dissolved minerals. Suitable for areas with good water quality but risk of microbial contamination.
  • UF (Ultrafiltration) Purifiers – Removes suspended particles, bacteria, and some viruses. Does not require electricity. Works on tap water pressure.
  • Gravity-Based Purifiers – No electricity required, uses gravity to pass water through filters. Ideal for rural areas or as backup.
  • Hot and Cold Water Dispensers – Tabletop or freestanding units that provide instant hot and cold purified water. Perfect for offices, hostels, and homes.

Installation and Maintenance Tips

For RO and UV purifiers, professional installation is essential. Ensure adequate space under the sink or on the countertop. The purifier should be installed near a power outlet and a water source. Replace pre-filters every 6-12 months and RO membranes every 2-3 years depending on usage and water quality. Clean the storage tank every 3 months with mild detergent. For gravity purifiers, clean the filter candles monthly. For water dispensers, descale the heating element every 6 months in areas with hard water.

Frequently Asked Questions About Water Purifiers

Which water purifier is best for Kathmandu's tap water?
Kathmandu's tap water typically has moderate to high TDS (200-500 ppm) and may contain bacteria. A RO+UV+UF purifier with TDS control, like Kent Grand Plus or Kent Sapphire, is recommended. These remove dissolved impurities and kill microorganisms while retaining essential minerals.
What is the difference between RO and UV water purifiers?
RO (Reverse Osmosis) removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, and chemicals, reducing TDS. UV (Ultraviolet) kills bacteria and viruses but does not remove dissolved impurities. For most Nepali households, a combination of RO+UV provides complete safety. If your water has low TDS (below 200 ppm), UV alone may suffice.
How often should I replace the filters in my water purifier?
Pre-filters and sediment filters should be replaced every 6-12 months. Carbon filters every 12 months. RO membranes every 2-3 years. UV lamps every 12-24 months. Most models have filter change indicators. Regular replacement ensures water quality and purifier longevity.
Do water purifiers work without electricity?
UV and RO purifiers require electricity to operate the UV lamp and booster pump. Gravity-based and UF purifiers work without electricity, using tap water pressure or gravity. Forbes and some Kent models offer non-electric options. For areas with unreliable power, consider a gravity purifier as backup.
What warranty do you offer on water purifiers?

Health Benefits of Purified Water

Drinking purified water reduces the risk of waterborne diseases like diarrhea, typhoid, and hepatitis. RO purifiers remove heavy metals (lead, arsenic, mercury) that can cause long-term health issues. UV purification eliminates bacteria and viruses without adding chemicals. By investing in a quality water purifier, you protect your family's health and reduce reliance on plastic bottled water, which is better for the environment and your wallet.

Energy Efficiency and Operating Costs

Modern water purifiers are highly energy-efficient. A typical RO purifier consumes 20-40 watts during operation, costing approximately Rs 50-100 per month in electricity. UV purifiers consume even less (15-25 watts). Filter replacement costs vary by model but average Rs 2,000-4,000 per year. Compared to buying bottled water (Rs 20-30 per liter), a home purifier pays for itself within months.
