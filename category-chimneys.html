Complete Chimney Buying Guide for Nepali Kitchens

A kitchen chimney helps remove smoke, grease, and cooking odors so your kitchen stays cleaner and more comfortable. For homes in Kathmandu where compact kitchens are common, choosing the right chimney size and suction capacity is very important.

At Shisham Homes, we focus on practical chimney models with reliable motors, easy controls, and service support. Whether you cook light meals or heavy daily frying and tadka, our collection includes options that fit both apartment kitchens and larger family kitchens.

What to check before buying

  • Suction power: For frequent frying and masala cooking, higher suction helps faster smoke extraction.
  • Size: 60cm chimneys generally suit compact hob sizes; 90cm is better for wider cooktops.
  • Filter style: Baffle filters are durable and easy to maintain for Indian/Nepali cooking patterns.
  • Auto-clean feature: Reduces manual cleaning effort and supports long-term performance.

Installation and maintenance tips

  • Keep proper gap between cooktop and chimney hood as suggested by technicians.
  • Clean visible oil residue regularly to maintain suction and improve lifespan.
  • Use original ducting and professional installation for safer operation.
  • Schedule periodic checks if you cook heavily every day.
