Solo vs Grill vs Convection – Which type suits your cooking needs?

Solo microwaves are basic units that only use microwave radiation to heat and cook. They are perfect for reheating, defrosting, and simple cooking like popcorn or heating milk. They are the most affordable.

Grill microwaves add a heating element that broils food from above, ideal for grilled dishes, toast, and crispy toppings. They cannot bake.

Convection microwaves have a built-in fan and heating element that circulates hot air, allowing baking and roasting – just like a conventional oven. They are the most versatile and can replace a traditional oven for most households.

Nepal expert pick: For most Nepali families, a convection microwave (like the Samsung CE76JD-B1 or Panasonic NN-CT645) offers the best value – you can bake cakes, roast chicken, and grill paneer all in one appliance.

Choosing the right capacity (litres)

Capacity determines how much food you can cook at once. For a family of 2-3, 20-25 litres is sufficient. For larger families or frequent baking, choose 25-30 litres. Larger ovens also accommodate bigger dishes like whole chicken or large pizza.

  • 1-2 persons: 17-20 L (solo or grill)
  • 3-4 persons: 21-25 L (convection recommended)
  • 5+ persons or heavy baking: 27-32 L (convection)

Power consumption and efficiency

Microwave ovens consume between 700W and 2200W depending on type and usage. Solo models use around 700-900W, grill models use additional power for the heating element, and convection models use the highest power for baking. However, cooking times are shorter, offsetting total energy use. Look for inverter models – they provide consistent power and consume up to 15% less electricity than conventional units. In Nepal, with rising electricity costs, an inverter microwave can save you money over time.

Maintenance tips for longer life

Wipe the interior after each use to prevent food buildup. Use a microwave-safe bowl with water and lemon to steam clean the interior monthly. Avoid metal utensils or containers with metallic trim. Never run the microwave empty – it can damage the magnetron.
