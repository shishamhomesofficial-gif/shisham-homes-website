Installation and Maintenance Tips for Samsung Refrigerators

To get the best performance from your Samsung refrigerator, ensure proper ventilation by leaving at least 4 inches of space on all sides. Place the refrigerator on a level surface to prevent door alignment issues. Clean the condenser coils every 6 months to maintain efficiency. For direct cool models, defrost when ice buildup exceeds 5mm. Set the temperature between 2 to 4 degrees Celsius for the fresh food compartment. Avoid overloading and keep the door closed as much as possible.
