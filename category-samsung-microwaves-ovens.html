Installation and Maintenance Tips for Samsung Microwaves

Place your Samsung microwave on a stable, level surface with at least 4 inches of ventilation space on all sides. Do not block the air vents. For convection models, allow the oven to cool down after baking before cleaning. Clean the ceramic enamel interior with a soft cloth and mild detergent after each use. Avoid using abrasive cleaners or metal scouring pads. Use microwave-safe cookware only – never use metal containers or aluminum foil in microwave mode. For the grill and convection modes, use appropriate oven-safe dishes.
