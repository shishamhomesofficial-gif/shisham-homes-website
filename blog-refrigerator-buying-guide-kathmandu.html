Refrigerator Buying Guide for Kathmandu Homes (2026)

A refrigerator should match your family size, kitchen space, and daily routine. Start by selecting the right capacity range and then compare cooling consistency and service support.

For small families, compact and mid-size models are often enough. For larger households, double-door models with strong cooling recovery are better.

Always measure door access, kitchen depth, and nearby ventilation space before delivery day to avoid installation issues.
