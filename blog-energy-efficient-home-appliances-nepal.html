Energy Efficient Home Appliances in Nepal: A Practical 2026 Buying Guide

Selecting efficient appliances helps reduce monthly electricity costs while improving long-term reliability. At Shisham Homes, we recommend checking power ratings, usable capacity, and warranty coverage before purchase.

Focus on inverter refrigerators, efficient microwaves, and front-load washing machines with lower power usage for daily operation.

Compare actual household usage instead of only sticker prices. A product with better efficiency usually gives better total value over 3-5 years.
