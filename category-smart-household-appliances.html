Frequently Asked Questions About Household Appliances

Which washing machine is best for Nepali homes – front load or top load?
Front-load washing machines are more water-efficient, gentler on clothes, and can be stacked with dryers. Top-load machines are generally more affordable, have shorter wash cycles, and are easier to load and unload. For most Nepali families, a 7-8kg top-load automatic washing machine offers the best value. Visit our showroom to see both types.
What capacity water purifier do I need for a family of 4?
A water purifier with 8-10 liters of storage capacity is sufficient for a family of 4. For larger families or heavy water usage, consider 15-20 liter models. Kent and CG offer various capacities to suit different household sizes.
How often should I replace the vacuum cleaner bag or filter?
For bag-type vacuum cleaners, replace the dust bag when it is about two-thirds full or every 2-3 months with regular use. HEPA filters should be replaced every 6-12 months depending on usage. Our service team can supply genuine replacement parts.
What size room heater do I need for a medium-sized bedroom?
For a standard Nepali bedroom of 150-200 square feet, a 800-1200 watt room heater provides adequate warmth. Look for models with thermostat control and oscillation for even heat distribution. Himstar and Kenstar offer reliable options.

Water purifiers and geysers have 1 year warranty with additional years on specific components like compressors and tanks. Full terms are provided with each product.

Maintenance Tips for Long Appliance Life

  • Washing Machines: Clean the detergent drawer and drum monthly. Leave the door open after use to prevent mold growth. Check hoses for leaks annually.
  • Vacuum Cleaners: Empty the dust container or replace bags regularly. Clean or replace filters as recommended. Check the brush roll for tangled hair and threads.
  • Water Purifiers: Replace filters and membranes according to the schedule provided. Clean the storage tank every 2-3 months. Schedule annual service checks.
  • Geysers: Flush the tank annually to remove sediment buildup. Check the pressure relief valve and anode rod for corrosion.
  • Deep Freezers: Defrost when ice buildup exceeds 5mm. Clean condenser coils every 6 months. Keep the freezer at least 4 inches away from walls for ventilation.
