Microwave Oven Buying Tips for Nepali Homes

When selecting a microwave oven, consider these important factors:

  • Capacity: 20 to 25 liters is suitable for most Nepali families of 2 to 4 members. Larger families may prefer 28 to 32 liter models for cooking bigger portions.
  • Convection Feature: Convection microwaves are best for baking traditional items like cakes, cookies, pizza, and even some Nepali snacks. If you enjoy baking or roasting, convection is essential.
  • Wattage: Higher wattage (800W to 1000W) means faster and more even cooking. Look for at least 800W for satisfactory performance.
  • Key Features: Child lock for safety, pre-set regional menus, auto-cook programs, defrost settings, and energy-saving inverter technology are valuable additions.
  • Interior Material: Stainless steel interiors are more durable, easier to clean, and resistant to scratches and odors compared to ceramic or painted cavities.

We recommend Panasonic's inverter models for precise temperature control and even heating, while Samsung's ceramic enamel cavities offer superior scratch resistance and easy cleaning. For budget-conscious buyers, WEBOR provides reliable performance at accessible price points.

Frequently Asked Questions About Microwaves and Ovens

Which is better for Nepali cooking – convection microwave or OTG?
A convection microwave combines microwave speed with oven baking capabilities, saving both counter space and time. An OTG (oven toaster grill) lacks microwave functions, making convection microwaves more versatile for daily cooking, reheating, and baking. For most Nepali households, a convection microwave is the superior choice.

Can I use metal utensils in a microwave oven?
Metal utensils should never be used in solo or grill microwave modes as they can cause sparks and damage the appliance. However, when using convection mode (operating as an oven), metal bakeware is acceptable. Always consult your product manual before using any cookware.
What is the warranty period on Panasonic and Samsung microwaves in Nepal?
Most models come with a 1 year comprehensive warranty covering parts and labor, plus an additional 5 years warranty on the magnetron (the core component that generates microwaves). Complete warranty terms are clearly mentioned on each product page and included with your purchase.
How do I clean and maintain my microwave oven?
Regular cleaning extends the life of your microwave. Wipe spills immediately with a soft cloth. For deep cleaning, heat a bowl of water with lemon juice for 3 minutes, then wipe the interior easily. Never use abrasive cleaners or steel wool on the interior surfaces.
