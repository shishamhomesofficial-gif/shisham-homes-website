Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Appliances

Which microwave is best for Nepali cooking – solo, grill, or convection?
For most Nepali households, a convection microwave offers the best value as it can reheat, grill, and bake. This allows you to prepare traditional baked items, roast vegetables, and even bake cakes. Solo microwaves are sufficient if you only need reheating and defrosting. Grill microwaves add the ability to toast and grill.
What is the power consumption of an induction cooker?
Induction cookers typically consume 1200 to 2000 watts depending on the power setting. However, they are more energy-efficient than gas or electric coil stoves because heat is generated directly in the pan. For average daily use of 1-2 hours, electricity costs are reasonable and often lower than gas.
What size kitchen chimney do I need for a Nepali kitchen?
For a standard Nepali kitchen with 2-3 burners, a 60cm to 90cm chimney with 1000-1200 m3/hr suction capacity is sufficient. Auto-clean models are recommended for convenience. Measure your cooktop size and kitchen layout before purchasing.
What wattage mixer grinder is suitable for grinding dry spices?
For grinding dry spices like coriander, cumin, and cinnamon, a mixer grinder with at least 750 watts motor power is recommended. Higher wattage (800-1000 watts) provides better grinding performance for tough ingredients. Bosch and Faber offer powerful models suitable for Indian and Nepali spice grinding.
What warranty do you offer on kitchen appliances?
Most kitchen appliances come with 1 year comprehensive manufacturer warranty. Microwaves have additional warranty on the magnetron (up to 5 years). Mixer grinders typically have 5 years warranty on the motor. Complete warranty terms are provided with each product.

Energy Efficiency Tips for Kitchen Appliances

  • Microwaves: Use microwave-safe cookware and cover food to reduce cooking time. Defrost food in the refrigerator before microwaving.
  • Induction Cookers: Use flat-bottomed, ferromagnetic cookware for maximum efficiency. Match pan size to the cooking zone.
  • Mixer Grinders: Cut ingredients into small pieces before grinding. Do not overload the jar. Run the motor in short bursts for hard ingredients.
  • Electric Kettles: Only boil the amount of water you need. Descale regularly to maintain heating efficiency.
  • Air Fryers: Preheat only when necessary. Shake the basket halfway through cooking for even results without opening frequently.

Maintenance Tips for Long Appliance Life

  • Microwaves: Clean spills immediately. Wipe interior with mild detergent weekly. Never operate when empty.
  • Induction Cookers: Clean the glass surface after each use with soft cloth. Avoid using abrasive cleaners or metal scrubbers.
  • Mixer Grinders: Clean jars and blades immediately after use. Do not immerse the motor base in water. Check carbon brushes annually.
  • Kitchen Chimneys: Clean filters every 2-3 weeks. For auto-clean models, run the cleaning cycle as recommended. Replace charcoal filters every 6 months for recirculation mode.
  • Air Fryers: Wash the basket and pan after each use. Wipe the heating element area with a damp cloth. Do not use metal utensils on non-stick surfaces.
